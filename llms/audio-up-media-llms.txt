# Audio Up Media

> Los Angeles based podcast studio, music company and audio entertainment network founded in 2020 by Jared Gutstadt. Audio Up develops scripted and unscripted audio series, original music IP and branded audio campaigns, and adapts that intellectual property into television, film and live formats. It is a content studio, not a software platform.

Audio Up Media publishes **no public API, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable specification**. This
profile is maintained by API Evangelist from the company's public marketing site only. Probed on
2026-08-06: `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/developers`, `/api`, `/llms.txt` and
the `/.well-known/` discovery paths all return 404 on www.audioup.com, and `api.`, `developer.` and
`docs.audioup.com` do not resolve in DNS. Shows are distributed through third-party podcast platforms.
